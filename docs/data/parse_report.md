## Known limitations

- Old SC volumes keep their OCR errors and marginal notes (dates, case names) inline; see `ocr_noise` above. Heavily damaged running heads can survive (rare).
- About 40 digital-signature lines survive where the text layer merges a footnote citation and the stamp into one line (`2 Digitally Signed (2012) 1 SCC 656`); removing them would risk the citation.
- A line-initial `A` after a sentence end is kept (it may be the article); B-H margin letters are always stripped in documents that use them.
- Case-name running heads on alternate pages are dropped only when they recur on at least 30% of pages.
- Line structure is kept (one output line per source line); paragraphs are assembled in PLAN 3.3.
