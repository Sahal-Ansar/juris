## Notes

- Judgment chunk offsets index the 3.2 clean text; `para_start`/`para_end` are `paragraphs.seq`. Statute chunk offsets index `statute_sections.text`.
- Chunks never cross an opinion part or a front/headnote/body boundary, so some chunks at those boundaries are small (the 0-99 row).
- Many Supreme Court paragraphs exceed one chunk (long quotations), hence the share of `sentences` windows; each window's header names the paragraph and the part (`¶ 12 (part 2/3)`).
- Old reports have inferred paragraphs (3.3), which are layout blocks rather than the court's paragraphs; their headers say `(unnumbered)`.
