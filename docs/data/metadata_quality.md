## Known limitations

- HC disposition: AWS leaves it empty for most Delhi/Bombay judgments and KanoonGPT adds little; it is not inferred from the judgment text.
- HC citations: Delhi/Bombay judgments before their neutral-citation schemes carry none; no reporter citations exist in the metadata.
- HC headnotes: none in either source.
- `author` is the AWS card's starred judge, or the sole judge of a one-judge bench; unmarked multi-judge benches stay `None`.
- Judgments missing from the KanoonGPT revision (the most recent ones) have AWS and text fields only.
- Old SCR bench lines are OCR-damaged; they only vote on bench strength and fill `judges` when no metadata source lists the bench.
